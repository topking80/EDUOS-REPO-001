# EduOS 32.4.0: mobile role apps, real transport, real Christie, Comms Hub

What was asked: build the mobile-first role apps from the Series B deck (pages
26-34, storyboards on pages 28-29), make transport and the Christie tutor real,
and add a communications hub. Keep RLS, OIDC/CSRF, audit, FERPA, per-school
branding, rate limits and axe at 0 violations. Operator actions are in
`docs/UPGRADING-32.4.0.md`.

Hard rules kept in this release:
- **No GPS or location tracking of students.** Transport records only
  driver-entered check-in events at named stops. The edge still sends
  `Permissions-Policy: geolocation=()`, and the app never asks for location.
- **No student personal data to an outside provider.** Christie uses a local,
  deterministic tutor by default and makes no network calls. The optional
  external adapter is off unless two env vars are set, and its request
  carries no ids, names, school, grade or email.
- **No real email, SMS or push sends.** Those channels are adapters that are
  off by default. The only shipped adapter besides `off` is `log`, a dry run.
- **No student-to-adult private messages.** Students read announcements only.

## 1. Mobile role apps (`/app/`)
- Installable PWA: its own manifest (`/app/manifest.webmanifest`, and the
  per-school `/api/branding/manifest?app=mobile`), maskable icon and a service
  worker that caches only the app shell. It never caches `/api` or `/auth`.
- Dark, phone-first layout with bottom tabs, built from the deck storyboards.
  The school's mascot and colors come from the published branding. Colors are
  adjusted for contrast against the dark background.
- Sign-in goes through the existing OIDC flow (`/auth/login?next=/app/`).
  `/app/` was added to the return-path allow-list. Drivers always land in
  `/app/`.
- Tabs per role:
  - **Student:** Home (mastery ring, today's bus, news), Christie, My path,
    News. Grade K-2 learners get a simpler, larger home.
  - **Teacher:** Classes, Grading queue, Roster, Christie copilot (computed
    from mastery and sessions, labelled "Not generated by AI", with session
    review), Messages.
  - **Family:** Children, Arrival (bus check-ins for linked children only),
    Messages, Alerts.
  - **Administrator (principal or superintendent):** Overview, Transport
    (routes, runs and check-ins), Messages, Alerts. IT admins are pointed to
    the desktop site.
  - **Bus driver (new `driver` role):** Route (today's run, stop by stop),
    Messages, Alerts.
- No inline scripts. The CSP is unchanged.

## 2. Transport
- Migration 012 adds `transport_routes`, `transport_stops`,
  `transport_rider_assignments`, `transport_runs` and `transport_checkins`.
  Check-ins are append-only (an update/delete trigger), FORCE RLS.
- A driver sees only routes assigned to them, starts a run, records
  "boarded", "got off" or "not riding today" per rider per stop, and completes the
  run. Guardians see check-ins for their own linked children only. Principals
  see their school, superintendents the district.
- Every check-in sends the rider's guardians an in-app alert, for example
  "Ava boarded Bus 7 at Maple Ave & 3rd St".
- APIs: `GET /api/transport/today`, `POST /api/transport/runs`,
  `POST /api/transport/runs/:runId/checkins` (rate limited),
  `POST /api/transport/runs/:runId/complete`, `GET/POST /api/transport/routes`,
  `POST /api/transport/routes/:routeId/riders`. Every write is audited.

## 3. Christie
- Tables: `christie_skills`, `christie_items`, `christie_sessions`,
  `christie_turns` and `student_skill_mastery`.
- **Hint first:** a wrong answer unlocks the next hint. "Just tell me the
  answer" is refused, and the refusal steers back to a hint. The answer is
  never sent to the browser. Pupils read items only through a SECURITY
  DEFINER function that leaves out the answer.
- **Approved content only:** a skill is used only after a principal or
  district role approves it (`POST /api/christie/skills/:id/approve`).
- Messages asking for an adult, or mentioning safety, get a fixed "please talk
  to a trusted adult" reply. The session is flagged
  (and audited) and listed first in the teacher copilot.
- Every turn is stored on the server and audited. Teachers on the roster read
  sessions and mark them reviewed (`/api/christie/review`).
- **Providers:** `local` (default; deterministic retrieval over the approved
  lesson text) or `openai_compatible`, used only when
  `CHRISTIE_PROVIDER=openai_compatible` and
  `CHRISTIE_ALLOW_EXTERNAL_PROVIDER=true`. Learner text is redacted (emails,
  URLs, phone numbers, student numbers, long digit runs, known names). A reply
  that leaks the answer, fails or times out is replaced by the local reply.
- Mastery is a simple estimate from recent correct first tries.

## 4. Comms Hub
- Tables: `comms_announcements`, `comms_announcement_reads`, `comms_threads`,
  `comms_participants`, `comms_messages`, `comms_message_reads`,
  `comms_reports` and `notification_outbox`.
- **Announcements:** principals post to their school, superintendents to the
  district, teachers to their own class only. Read tracking and removal are
  included.
- **Threads with read receipts:**
  - `family` threads must include a guardian and a teacher or principal, and
    concern the guardian's own child.
  - `staff` threads include staff only.
  - Participants must come from the caller's allowed contacts.
  - Pupils cannot open or join threads; `GET /api/comms/threads` returns
    `policy: students_read_announcements_only`.
- **Moderation:** any participant can report a message. Principals are
  notified, then hide or dismiss it with a reason; a hidden message's body is
  withheld. They can also lock a thread. Every action is audited.
- **Notification outbox:** in-app rows are delivered at once
  (`GET /api/notifications`). External channels get a generic line with no
  student text and are queued only when their adapter is enabled.
  `src/notify-dispatch.js` (`make notify`, owner role) hands them to the
  adapter; with `log` it records a dry run.

## 5. Security, privacy and accessibility
- All new tables use FORCE RLS with tenant isolation and RESTRICTIVE
  per-table policies. The runtime role is not the table owner.
- CSRF is required on every write. There are new rate limits for Christie,
  Comms and transport check-ins.
- FERPA export now includes transport assignments and check-ins, Christie
  sessions, turns and mastery, and comms threads. `eduos_purge_student`
  removes the student's transport, Christie, mastery and student-linked
  comms thread rows.
- axe-core WCAG 2.1 A/AA: 0 violations on all 22 mobile screens scanned.

## Not verified, or still demo
- The email, SMS and push adapters are `off` or `log` only. No real provider
  is wired up.
- The external Christie provider was tested only with unit tests against the
  request body and redaction. It has not run against a real endpoint.
- Mastery is a heuristic, not a calibrated model. The teacher copilot is
  computed.
- "Today" for transport runs uses the database's `current_date` (UTC in the
  dev stack). Late-evening US runs can roll over to the next day.
- `db:retention` does not yet cover the new tables.
- The report and lock reasons use the browser's `prompt()` dialog. An in-app
  dialog would be better.
- Push requires a real VAPID/APNs/FCM sender; the service worker does not
  subscribe.
