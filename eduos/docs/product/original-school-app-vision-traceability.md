# Original School App Vision — Requirements Traceability

**Source:** Mathew C. Pennington, *School App.docx* (9 pages), supplied in this project.
**Repository audited:** `topking80/EDUOS-REPO-001`, `main` at the time of review.
**Purpose:** Preserve the original product vision as a first-class requirements baseline and distinguish implemented capabilities from partial coverage and unverified gaps.
**Important:** This is a source-to-code gap assessment, not a claim that every requirement is complete. A module or route existing does not by itself prove production readiness, user-interface completeness, or safe operation.

## Status definitions

- **Implemented (evidence found):** A corresponding schema, API, client, and/or test exists in the inspected repository. End-to-end deployment and operational readiness still require their own verification.
- **Partial:** A related capability exists, but one or more source requirements, roles, interfaces, integrations, or reliability guarantees are not evidenced.
- **Gap / not evidenced:** No corresponding implementation was found in the inspected files. This is not proof that no prototype exists elsewhere.
- **Needs policy decision:** The source proposes a capability that creates material privacy, safety, legal, or operational risk; define a safe, lawful version before implementation.

## Executive assessment

EduOS already has substantial foundations for learning, family access, communications, attendance and transportation, emergency roll-call, school app licensing/marketplace, commerce, branding, mobile clients, tenant isolation, and a school-edge deployment. It does **not** follow that the full original vision is complete.

The largest unclosed areas identified in this review are: a genuine cross-school/federated learner identity and portable history; a full student timeline and sealed time-capsule feature; visitor management; comprehensive schedule/substitute workflows; secure visitor/police interoperability; local-first offline operation with tested synchronization across all workflows; global teacher/student collaboration; donations; full camera/document-scanner and guest-speaker workflows; and a privacy-preserving alternative to continuous location tracking.

## Implementation order

1. **Complete Timeline & Time Capsule safely** in the active feature branch: migration, routes, UI, media/consent integration, worker, grants, RLS, integration tests, and operational documentation.
2. **Cross-school identity and portable learner record design:** federation trust, stable identifier lifecycle, data portability, consent, revocation, corrections, and legal review. This is a prerequisite for the original K–12 cross-school promise.
3. **Visitor management and unified schedules/substitute workflows.**
4. **Local-first reliability:** prove school-site server operations without WAN, queue/replay safety, sync conflict handling, local emergency operation, backup/restore, and upgrade/rollback.
5. **Close emergency interoperability gaps:** device/alarm integrations, notification escalation, agency-approved police workflows, and realistic drills.
6. **Complete parent/teacher/employee experiences:** consolidated family view, contact relay, teacher resources/guest speakers, media capture/consent, and role-complete mobile UX.
7. **Marketplace, bookstore, donations, and school settlement:** signed app distribution, sandboxing, privacy reviews, fee disclosure, refund/chargeback handling, and accounting.
8. **Global collaboration and research participation:** defer until safeguarding, identity federation, moderation, and research governance are approved.

## Release acceptance rule

Do not mark a vision item complete solely because a database table or route exists. Each item must have:
- a working web/mobile user journey for every intended role;
- server-side authorization and tenant/school-scope enforcement;
- migration and least-privilege runtime grants where applicable;
- automated tests for normal, denied, cross-tenant, stale/offline, replay, and failure cases;
- privacy, retention, accessibility, and operational documentation;
- verified deployment/monitoring evidence where the feature depends on external devices, providers, or school infrastructure.
