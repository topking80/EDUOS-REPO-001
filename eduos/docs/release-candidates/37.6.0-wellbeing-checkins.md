# 37.6.0 candidate: Well-being check-ins and the crisis hook

- Opt-in check-ins: off until a superintendent turns them on, then only for
  learners whose guardian opted in under the current notice. A new notice
  version requires a fresh opt-in.
- Read by counselors only (the existing `counselor` role gets
  `wellbeing:queue`). Principals, teachers and the district office have no
  access. Enforced by RLS.
- Crisis words in a check-in or a Christie chat show the 988 Lifeline, alert
  the school's counselors at once, and open an URGENT item in the existing
  counseling queue (no identifiers). Asking to talk opens a high-priority
  item.
- No scores, predictions or rankings: two plain queue buckets by a published
  rule, then oldest first.
- Migration `060_wellbeing_checkins.sql` (renumber if 051-057 land first).
  New setting: `WELLBEING_RATE_LIMIT_PER_MIN`.
- Tests: `test/wellbeing-37.6.test.js`, `test/integration/wellbeing.test.js`.
  See `docs/WELLBEING.md`.
