# 37.7.0 candidate: Commerce (sandbox only)

- Paid school-event tickets:
  - inventory holds;
  - guardian-only payment (pupils never pay);
  - receipts;
  - QR check-in with hashed codes;
  - whole-order refunds that are blocked once a ticket is scanned.
- Marketplace purchase on the 37.6 fee hook: the superintendent prices a
  listing and pays for an approved license; 5% marketplace fee and vendor
  payable.
- Revenue split ledger:
  - integer cents, deterministic rounding;
  - deck model by default: 3% network fee, net 85% school / 10% district /
    5% state fund;
  - append-only; every settlement and refund is an EDAL entry.
- `PaymentProvider` with one adapter, `sandbox` (no keys, no network).
  `COMMERCE_PAYMENT_PROVIDER` defaults to `none`; anything else but
  `sandbox` stops the server. The superintendent must also turn commerce on.
- Fix: the runtime role can now update ticket status and codes (narrow
  column grants plus transition triggers), so gate check-in works.
- Migration `065_commerce_sandbox.sql`. The open FCM device push PR (#52)
  must renumber past 065.
- Web:
  - families: Tickets and Receipt screens;
  - leaders: Ticketing & revenue screen.
- Flutter: Tickets screen for families and learners.
- Tests:
  - `test/commerce-37.7.test.js`;
  - `test/integration/commerce.test.js`;
  - `mobile/flutter/test/commerce_tickets_test.dart`.
- See `docs/COMMERCE-SANDBOX.md`, including what must be decided before real
  money: processor, PCI, payouts, sales tax, legal review.
